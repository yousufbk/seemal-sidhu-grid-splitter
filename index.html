Seemal Sidhu — Puzzle Grid Splitter

One big picture, cut into Instagram posts using the proven bleed method. Everything runs in your browser — your picture is never uploaded anywhere.

How to use:
1. Select a picture
2. Choose layout and mode
3. Drag the picture to position it. Scroll / pinch / use + and − to make it bigger or smaller
4. Click “Create Tiles”
5. Post the tiles on Instagram in order: 01 first, then 02, 03…

The proven bleed method — built in automatically:
Your picture is sliced into 1010 x 1350 sections. That is the part Instagram actually shows in the profile grid.
Every tile is exported at 1080 x 1350, with 35 px of extra picture added on the left and on the right, taken from the neighbouring section. At the outer edges, the extra is made by extending the edge of the picture.
When you post, Instagram's grid crop eats that extra 35 px — not your picture — so the sections join perfectly and nothing looks cut. Open any single post and you will see the full 1080 px; the extra is hidden only in grid view.
Safe zone (red dashed) is a separate guide: keep faces, text and logos inside it, away from the cut lines.

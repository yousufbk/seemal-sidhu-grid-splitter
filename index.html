Seemal Sidhu — Puzzle Grid Splitter

One big picture, cut into equal Instagram posts. Everything runs in your browser — your picture is never uploaded anywhere.

How to use:
1. Select a picture
2. Choose layout, format and mode
3. Drag the picture to position it. Scroll / pinch / use + and − to make it bigger or smaller
4. Click “Create Tiles”
5. Post the tiles on Instagram in order: 01 first, then 02, 03…

Exact grid buffer (verified): the profile grid shows every post at 3:4. 3:4 format = 0 px cropped. 4:5 format = 35 px left + 35 px right hidden in grid view only (the centre 1010 px shows; the full post still shows when opened). Square = 135 px per side. Keep faces, text and logos inside the red dashed safe zone (80 px from every tile edge). In 4:5, the orange lines mark the 35 px that will disappear in the grid — background only there.

Drag to move  •  Scroll or pinch to zoom  •  White lines = cut lines  •  Red dashed = safe zone
